# Femcart — Premium Women's Intimate Apparel & Lifestyle E-commerce Platform

## Project Overview

Femcart is a premium online shopping platform dedicated to women's intimate apparel, shapewear, activewear, and essential lifestyle products. The platform is designed to provide a comfortable, elegant, and confidence-inspiring shopping experience by combining sophisticated visual design with intelligent product discovery, personalized recommendations, and a seamless purchasing journey.

Where women can discover products that support their comfort, confidence, and everyday lifestyle.
